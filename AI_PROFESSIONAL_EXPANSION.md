## 2. The 5 Priority Autonomous Production Systems

### Priority 01 — AI Editing Evaluation System (Objective Quality Scoring)
Unlike subjective intuition, the evaluation system scores edits across 6 quantifiable metrics:
1. **Pacing Continuity Index (PCI):** Ratio of cut lengths relative to musical rhythm.
2. **Beat Synchronization Precision (BSP):** Cut time deviation from acoustic transient peaks ($\le \pm 35\text{ms}$).
3. **Hook Retention Score (HRS):** Energy and contrast gradient in the initial $0.0\text{s} - 3.0\text{s}$.
4. **Visual Saliency Retention:** Ensuring subjects stay within active focal zones.
5. **Broadcast Audio Compliance:** Integrated Loudness target $-14.0\text{ LUFS} \pm 1\text{ LUFS}$, True Peak $\le -1.0\text{ dBFS}$.
6. **Artifact Freedom Score:** 0 black frames, 0 frozen frames, 0 epileptic strobe hazards.

### Priority 02 — Multi-Agent Collaboration Council
Editing requires distinct cognitive specializations. The platform runs 5 concurrent AI Agents:
- **Vision Director Agent:** Identifies combat moments, camera movements, and frames.
- **Audio Master Agent:** Analyzes transients, manages ducking, and places impact sound effects.
- **Narrative Story Agent:** Governs the dramatic arc (Hook → Build-up → Climax → Outro).
- **VFX & Motion Agent:** Applies velocity easing, Impact Frames, and radial speed lines.
- **Quality Control (QC) Inspector:** Audits cuts against broadcast-safe constraints.
- **Lead Executive Director:** Arbitrates disputes and determines final cut approvals.

### Priority 03 — Creative Decision Engine
Prevents repetitive edits by analyzing visual and narrative context:
- If a high-motion melee strike connects: triggers **Inverted Impact Frame (1-2 frames)** followed by **Sub-bass Drop**.
- If a sniper headshot occurs: triggers **Pre-impact Velocity Slow-mo (0.35x)** into **Radial Speed Blur**.
- If emotional dialogue plays: triggers **Audio Ducking (-12dB BGM)** and **Cinematic Vignette**.

### Priority 04 — Automatic Re-Edit Loop
If an edit scores below **88 / 100**, the system automatically:
1. Identifies specific failure points (e.g. cut 3 is 70ms off-beat; hook has low contrast).
2. Nudges cut boundaries to nearest acoustic transients.
3. Swaps weak B-roll for high-saliency candidate highlights.
4. Re-evaluates until score $\ge 88$ or 3 iterations are exhausted.

### Priority 05 — Professional Editing Intelligence
Incorporates classic cinematic and AMV principles:
- **Cutting on Action:** Hiding cuts inside character movement rather than static pauses.
- **Match Cutting:** Aligning directional vectors across scene boundaries.
- **Pacing Escalation:** Shortening clip duration exponentially towards the Climax.
